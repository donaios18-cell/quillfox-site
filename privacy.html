Quillfox for iPhone and Apple Watch

Privacy Policy

Effective 17 November 2026NG Hive LLCcontact@nghive.app

The short version

  • Quillfox has no account, no ads, no analytics and no server of its own. Transcripts stay on your iPhone.
  • Speech is turned into text by Apple’s speech recognition. Quillfox asks for it to run on the device; where your language cannot, iPhone may send the audio to Apple.
  • Nothing leaves the app unless you share it.
1

Who this covers

This policy covers the Quillfox iPhone app and its companion Apple Watch app, published by NG Hive LLC (“the developer”, “we”). It applies to everyone who installs the app.

2

What stays on your device

Quillfox has no server, no account and no sign-in. It contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting service. The app keeps this in its own storage on your iPhone:

  • your transcripts, with the date and the time of each phrase;
  • the folders you create and which transcript is in which;
  • your settings, such as the language to listen for and the text size.

The developer cannot read any of it.

3

Microphone and speech recognition

With your permission, Quillfox uses the microphone to hear the speech around you and passes it to Apple’s speech recognition to be turned into text. The sound is processed as it arrives; Quillfox does not save audio recordings — only the text.

Quillfox asks iPhone to recognise speech on the device whenever Apple supports your language that way, and then the audio does not leave the phone. For a language or an iOS version that cannot be recognised on the device, iPhone may send the audio to Apple to be recognised. That processing is done by Apple and is covered by Apple’s own privacy policy; the developer does not receive the audio or the text.

iOS may also download a speech model for a language the first time you use it. You can withdraw the microphone and speech recognition permissions at any time in Settings → Quillfox.

4

Apple Watch and the Lock Screen

If you use the Apple Watch app, the recognised text is sent between your iPhone and your paired watch so that it can be shown there. While recording, a Live Activity may show the latest phrase on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, where anyone who sees your locked iPhone can read it. You can switch Live Activities off in Settings.

5

Sharing

A transcript leaves the app only when you tap “Send text” or “Save file” and choose where it goes. What happens to it afterwards depends on the app or service you chose.

6

Keeping and deleting your data

You can delete any transcript or folder inside the app. Deleting Quillfox from your iPhone removes everything it stored. Because nothing is kept on our side, there is nothing for us to delete for you. If your iPhone backs up app data, copies may remain in your own backups under your own settings.

7

Children

Quillfox is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. The app collects no information at all.

8

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date. Material changes will also be mentioned in the App Store release notes.

9

Contact

Questions about privacy: contact@nghive.app.
