Start it from your wrist

Quillfox has an Apple Watch app and a button for the watch’s Control Centre. The words appear on the watch, while the iPhone stays in your pocket.

  1. Add the button once. On the watch, open Control Centre, tap +, then Quillfox under “From Your iPhone”. The app shows you how.
  2. Press it to start. Recording begins on the iPhone, even locked, and a Live Activity shows the latest phrase on the Lock Screen.
  3. Press again to stop. The transcript is waiting in the app.
Quillfox · Recording

The Control Centre button needs watchOS 26 on the watch. Starting from the app on the watch works while Quillfox is open on the iPhone.

Short answers

Does it work without internet?

Yes, for the languages your iPhone can recognise on the device. The first time you use a language, iOS may need to download its speech model.

Which languages can it recognise?

The ones Apple’s speech recognition supports on your iPhone. You pick the language to listen for on the main screen. The app itself speaks English, Russian, Spanish, German, French and Portuguese.

Does it tell speakers apart?

Not yet. Apple’s recogniser gives text without speaker labels, so a transcript is one running text with times.

Is it always accurate?

No automatic transcript is. Noise, accents and distance cause mistakes, so check anything important before you rely on it.

Can I record anyone I like?

Not necessarily. Some places require everyone’s consent to record or transcribe a conversation. Please tell people and follow the law where you are.

Android?

Not at the moment. Quillfox is for iPhone first, with Apple Watch alongside.

Needs iPhone with iOS 18 or later. Apple Watch features need watchOS 26.
